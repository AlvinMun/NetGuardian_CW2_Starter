and explain a meaningful part of the actual implementation.

- 0:00-0:45 — Introduction, team and NetGuardian objectives
- 0:45-2:00 — Task 1: topology, hosts, switches, controller and pingall
- 2:00-3:30 — Task 2: MAC learning, forwarding, policy tests and flow table
- 3:30-5:00 — Task 3: ICMP/TCP generation, monitoring CSV and two graphs
- 5:00-7:15 — Task 4: low-rate vs high-rate test, alert, DROP rule, legitimate
  connectivity, timeout and recovery
- 7:15-8:45 — Task 5: integrated workflow and five-run performance table
- 8:45-10:00 — Limitations, conclusion and individual contributions

Divide the sections among all actual group members. Show live terminal output and
explain your own code. Do not narrate unverified or simulated results as real.
